FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

What commercial property maintenance services does CRC Maintenance provide?

CRC Maintenance provides rope access, high-level building maintenance, cladding and façade cleaning, roof cleaning and maintenance, DOFF steam cleaning, gutter and window cleaning, Planned Preventative Maintenance, bird proofing, drone roof surveys and pressure washing for commercial properties.

Who does CRC Maintenance work with?

We work with facilities management companies, managing agents, commercial landlords, estates teams, main contractors, local authorities and organisations responsible for commercial and public buildings.

Where does CRC Maintenance operate?

CRC Maintenance is based in Manchester and serves the North West and specified wider regional locations. Nationwide delivery is available for confirmed specialist rope access, DOFF steam cleaning and high-level projects.

Can CRC Maintenance work as a specialist subcontractor?

Yes. We support facilities management providers and main contractors with defined specialist access, cleaning, inspection and external maintenance packages.

What information is needed for a quotation?

We need the property location, required service, work area, known access conditions, available photographs or survey information and the required timescale. A site or technical assessment may be needed before the scope is confirmed.

Can maintenance work be planned around an operational building?

We consider site operations during planning. The available scheduling and access approach depends on the building, work area, safety requirements and agreed scope.
